# Question word (13)

- was (Was ist das?) — what? (What is that?)
- wie (Wie heißen die Wörter in Ihrer Sprache?) — here: what (What are the words called in your language?)
- wer (Wer bist du?) — who? (Who are you?)
- welcher, welche, welches — which
- wo (Wo wohnen Sie?) — where (Where do you live?)
- woher (Woher kommst du?) — from where (From where are you?)
- wie viel, wie viele — how much
- was — what
- wie lange — how long
- wen — whom
- warum — why
- wohin — whereto
- wem — whom, who
