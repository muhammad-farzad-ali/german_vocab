# German A1 conjunctions — study table (14)

| ID | BASE_WORD | TYPE | WORD_ORDER | EN_MEANING | DE_SENTENCE | EN_SENTENCE_TRANSLATION |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | aber | coordinating | Satzklammer | but | Ich arbeite viel, aber ich bin zufrieden. | I work a lot, but I am satisfied. |
| 2 | aber | modal particle | emphasize | but really | Das ist aber schön! | That is really beautiful! |
| 3 | weil | subordinating | Verb am Ende | because | Ich bleibe, weil es regnet. | I stay because it is raining. |

| 10 | immer wenn | subordinating | Verb am Ende | whenever | Immer wenn ich koche, hilft sie. | Whenever I cook, she helps. |
