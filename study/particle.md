# German A1 particles — study table (10)

| ID | BASE_WORD | PARTICLE_TYPE | FUNCTION | EN_MEANING | DE_SENTENCE | EN_SENTENCE_TRANSLATION |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | bitte | politeness | soften a request | please | Bitte ein Glas Wasser. | A glass of water, please. |
| 2 | ja | answer particle | affirm | yes | Ja, sehr gern. | Yes, very much. |
| 3 | nein | answer particle | negate | no | Nein, danke. | No, thank you. |
| 4 | aber | modal particle | emphasize (impatience) | but | Jetzt aber schnell! | But quickly now! |
| 5 | ja | modal particle | soften (shared knowledge) | modal particle | Wir können ja morgen telefonieren. | We can call tomorrow, after all. |
| 6 | denn | modal particle | emphasize (curiosity) | then | Was denn? | Whatever is it? |
| 7 | doch | answer particle | contradict a negation | on the contrary | Doch, ich komme! | Yes, I am coming after all! |
| 8 | oder | modal particle | seek agreement | right | Das ist schrecklich, oder? | That is terrible, is not it? |
| 9 | mal | modal particle | soften a request | sometime | Komm mal her! | Come here a moment! |
| 10 | also | modal particle | introduce a topic | well | Also, die Jacke gefällt mir. | Well, I like the jacket. |
